Claim a plugin id

An id is first come, permanent, and never recycled. It names your plugin in every installed copy on every user's machine, so it is never released or reassigned — not even after a delisting, and not by us. Claim the id your plugin's manifest declares, and check the spelling before you submit.

Claiming registers the id to your account and makes you its owner. It does not list anything. Your plugin appears in the index once a release of it has been approved, and the first release of a new id always goes to human review — whatever your trust level. The whole path is on the publishing walkthrough.
